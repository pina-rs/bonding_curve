## [0.2.0](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/releases/tag/v0.2.0) (2026-10-12)

Grouped release for `bonding_curve`.

### What's new

#### Graduation works for launchpads that launch their own tokens

_Owner:_ [@ifiokjr](https://github.com/ifiokjr) · _Review:_ [PR #9](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/pull/9)

##### User impact

A partner who launches a token under their own launchpad can now graduate it to the open market: both LP payouts are merged into one payment to their wallet. Handing a launch to the unset default address is refused instead of locking that launch's creator earnings forever.

#### Mis-sent quote is no longer stranded

_Owner:_ [@ifiokjr](https://github.com/ifiokjr) · _Review:_ [PR #10](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/pull/10) · _Related issues:_ [#9](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/issues/9)

##### User impact

Quote tokens sent straight to a launch's vault used to sit above the launch's accounting forever. Anyone can now call `SweepQuoteDust` to split that stray balance between the token's creator and the launchpad by the configured fee share, before or after graduation, and creator handovers are visible in transaction logs.

#### The bonding curve has its permanent address

_Owner:_ [@ifiokjr](https://github.com/ifiokjr) · _Review:_ [PR #18](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/pull/18) · _Related issues:_ [#16](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/issues/16)

##### User impact

The bonding curve now lives at the address it will keep on mainnet, and launches graduate into the Pina AMM at its permanent address. Anything that saved the earlier test addresses needs to switch to the new ones. Security researchers can also find the project's private reporting channel directly from the deployed program.

#### A launch can no longer be handed to itself

_Owner:_ [@ifiokjr](https://github.com/ifiokjr) · _Review:_ [PR #16](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/pull/16)

##### User impact

Handing a launch's creator rights to the launch's own address is refused. Before this change, a creator could do that to make a completed launch impossible to move to the open market, which left buyers' funds stuck in it.

#### Cheaper trades on a smaller program

_Owner:_ [@ifiokjr](https://github.com/ifiokjr) · _Review:_ [PR #15](https://github.com/pina-rs/bonding_curve/pull/15)

##### User impact

Every buy and sell now costs about 830 and 760 fewer compute units, and the deployed program is about 11 KB (9%) smaller, which lowers its rent. Trades no longer revalidate the price curve stored in an immutable configuration, copy only the pricing fields they need out of it, and share one compiled copy of the slow wide-division and signer-seed routines instead of a copy per call site. Nothing about the math, fees, or account layout changed.
