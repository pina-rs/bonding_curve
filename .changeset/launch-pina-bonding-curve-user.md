# Run your own token launchpad on Solana

## User impact

You can now launch a token whose price rises along a curve you choose, from a classic single curve to a stepped schedule. Every launch has a fixed supply that can never grow, an opening fee that discourages bots, and earnings for the token's creator and the launchpad that hosts it. When a launch reaches its target, anyone can move it to an open market at exactly the price it reached, with its liquidity locked.
